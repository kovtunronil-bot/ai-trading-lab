=====================================================
  YOUR AI TRADING ROBOT — PLAIN-WORDS MANUAL
=====================================================

WHAT IS THIS?
  A robot on your PC that trades fake money ($100,000)
  across 9 markets (7 stocks + Bitcoin + Ethereum),
  improves its own strategies, and reports to your phone.

-----------------------------------------------------
FILES IN THIS FOLDER
-----------------------------------------------------
run-robot.bat ........ DOUBLE-CLICK to run the robot now
run-robot-night.bat .. silent version (used automatically at night)
run-report.bat ....... ask the AI to analyze the robot's diary
bot.py ............... the robot itself
brain.py ............. the self-improvement engine (24 strategies)
report.py ............ sends diary to Llama for weekly critique
keys.py .............. passwords (Alpaca keys + phone alert topic)

journal.csv .......... robot's diary: one line per market per run
lessons.txt .......... evolution history: every strategy change ever made
equity_history.csv ... daily scoreboard of your fake money
reports_history.md ... all past AI reports
config_*.json ........ current champion strategy per market

-----------------------------------------------------
WHEN DOES IT WORK? (Windows Task Scheduler)
-----------------------------------------------------
19:00 daily .... main run: evolve brain if stale + trade + phone alert
19:40 Sunday ... Llama writes weekly report
23:30 / 03:30 / 07:30 ... night patrols (alert only if a trade happens)

-----------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ THE ROBOT'S OUTPUT
-----------------------------------------------------
"entry needs : RSI 47 < 5"   -> it buys only in rare panic dips
"filters blocked entry"      -> signal fired but safety said no
"EXECUTED BUY/SELL"          -> a real paper order was placed

SAFETY LADDER (independent of AI - pure math):
  -5%  CAUTION .... no new buys; exits allowed; auto-recovers
  -10% HALT ....... sell everything; frozen until you run:
                      py bot.py resume
  -20% LOCKDOWN ... sell everything; frozen until you prove intent:
                      py bot.py resume LOCKDOWN
  Plus per-position stop-loss: any holding that falls -8%
  below its buy price is force-closed before strategies
  even get a vote.
